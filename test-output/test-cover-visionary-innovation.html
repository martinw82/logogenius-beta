Project Overview

Company introduction, mission, and vision

THUNDERFORGE is a forward-thinking technology company dedicated to creating powerful solutions that empower businesses to achieve more. Founded with a vision to democratize enterprise-grade tools, we have grown into a trusted partner for organizations seeking to transform their digital presence.

Logo Philosophy

Design thinking and core message

The THUNDERFORGE logo represents our core values of power and precision. The geometric forms suggest stability and forward momentum, while the color palette evokes trust and energy. The mark is designed to be memorable at any size, from app icons to billboards.

Color Usage Proportions

Follow the 60-30-10 rule for balanced color application:

  • 60% Primary: Backgrounds, major UI elements
  • 30% Secondary: Supporting elements, sections
  • 10% Accent: CTAs, highlights, emphasis

Color Accessibility

WCAG compliance and contrast guidelines

All color combinations meet WCAG AA standards for contrast. Primary text uses a 4.5:1 ratio against backgrounds, while large text achieves 3:1. Avoid using color alone to convey information.

Imagery & Photography

Visual direction for photography and illustration

Photography should feel authentic and aspirational. Use natural lighting, diverse subjects, and real-world contexts. Avoid generic stock imagery. Images should convey action, collaboration, and positive outcomes.

Graphic Elements

Icons, patterns, and decorative components

Geometric shapes derived from the logo can be used as background patterns or accent elements. Maintain consistent corner radii and line weights. Use these elements sparingly to avoid visual clutter.

THUNDERFORGE Brand Guidelines 11

Visual Style Guide

Spacing, grids, shadows, and visual standards

Maintain consistent spacing using an 8px grid system. Use subtle shadows (0 2px 8px rgba) for depth. Border radius should be consistent—4px for UI elements, 8px for cards. Keep designs clean and purposeful.

Usage Rules & Guidelines

Protecting your brand integrity

DO
  • Use approved brand colors
  • Maintain minimum clear space around logo
  • Use on solid, contrasting backgrounds
  • Scale proportionally
  • Use high-resolution files for print
DON'T
  • Stretch or distort the logo
  • Change logo colors arbitrarily
  • Place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Rotate or tilt the logo
  • Add effects like shadows or outlines

Clear Space Requirements

Always maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "x" in the logotype. This ensures the logo remains visible and uncluttered.

Minimum Size

  • Print: Minimum 25mm wide
  • Digital: Minimum 80px wide
  • Favicon: 16px × 16px (simplified version)

Color Variations

Use the appropriate logo version based on background:

  • Primary: Full color on white/light backgrounds
  • Reversed: White version on dark backgrounds
  • Single Color: Black or white for special applications
